AI for Earth: Case Studies

Explore real-world applications of artificial intelligence in environmental conservation and sustainability efforts worldwide.

Microsoft AI for Earth Initiative

Supporting projects using AI for global environmental challenges

Region

Global

Impact

Over 700 grantees in 100+ countries

Supported AI models for land cover mapping, agriculture yield prediction, climate modeling, and biodiversity monitoring.

Example Subproject:

Wild Me (USA): Uses AI-powered photo ID for wildlife like whale sharks and giraffes using pattern recognition to track individuals and fight illegal poaching.

Google's Project Loon + TensorFlow for Forest Protection

Using AI to detect illegal logging in real time

Region

Amazon Rainforest, Indonesia

How it works:

TensorFlow trained on acoustic signatures of chainsaws and trucks. Sensors placed in trees trigger alerts when illegal logging is detected.

Impact:

Real-time alerts to local forest patrols. Helped reduce logging by enabling faster response times.

PAWS (Protection Assistant for Wildlife Security)

AI for anti-poaching patrols

Region

Africa & Southeast Asia

Developed By

Harvard + USC

How it works:

Uses game theory and machine learning to optimize ranger patrols in wildlife reserves. Predicts poaching hotspots using past data.

Impact:

Significant reduction in poaching. Deployed across national parks in Uganda, Cambodia, and Malaysia.

IBM Green Horizons Project

AI for pollution control and renewable energy forecasting

Region

China

How it works:

AI models air quality and forecasts pollution up to 72 hours in advance. Helps urban planners and industries reduce emissions.

Impact:

Adopted by over 20 cities in China. Helped reduce air pollution levels in Beijing by informing government regulations.

BlueDot – AI for Environmental Health

AI predicting environmental health threats

Region

Global

Focus

Water & Disease

How it works:

Tracks environmental data, climate events, and water pollution. Alerts health agencies of potential disease outbreaks.

Case Study:

First to warn of COVID-19 outbreak in Wuhan (Dec 2019), linked to environmental triggers.

DeepMind + Google Data Centers

AI for energy efficiency in data centers

Region

Global

How it works:

DeepMind's RL (Reinforcement Learning) optimized cooling systems. Reduced energy used for cooling by 40%.

Impact:

Lower carbon footprint of large-scale tech infrastructure. Now applied to sustainable AI model training.

PEAT's Plantix (Germany/India)

AI for sustainable agriculture

Region

India, Sub-Saharan Africa

How it works:

Uses computer vision to diagnose crop diseases. Recommends eco-friendly treatments and water usage.

Impact:

Over 10 million farmers helped. Promotes sustainable pesticide use and water conservation.

Global Fishing Watch (Google + Oceana + SkyTruth)

Combatting illegal fishing and protecting marine life

Region

Global

How it works:

Uses AI and satellite imagery to track fishing vessels. Public platform shows real-time fishing activity.

Impact:

Helps governments and researchers monitor marine reserves. Curbs illegal, unreported, and unregulated fishing.

CarbonPlan

Transparency in carbon removal using AI

Region

USA / Global

How it works:

Open-source models for evaluating climate tech (like direct air capture). AI predicts effectiveness of carbon offsets and removals.

Impact:

Influences corporate sustainability decisions. Promotes credible, science-based climate action.
