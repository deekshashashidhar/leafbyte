Tech has a footprint you can measure

Every figure on LeafByte links to its source. How we choose sources.

415 TWh Electricity used by the world's data centres in 2024, about 1.5% of the global total

Source: IEA, Energy and AI (2025)

62 Mt E-waste produced in 2022; only 22.3% was documented as properly recycled

Source: Global E-waste Monitor (2024)

2.1–3.9% Estimated share of global greenhouse gas emissions from information and communication tech

Source: Freitag et al., Patterns (2021)
