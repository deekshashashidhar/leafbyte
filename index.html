Our Sustainable AI & Tech Solutions

Nine ways to shrink technology's footprint, each with one thing you can try today.

Efficient AI Models

Choose energy efficiency, not just accuracy. Smaller or distilled models such as DistilBERT often do the job with far less compute, and LoRA is a fine-tuning technique that updates only a few extra weights.

Try this today: test whether a smaller model meets your accuracy target before using the largest one.

Green Data Centers

Run data centres on wind, solar and hydropower, and cool them smarter. AI-controlled cooling cut the energy Google used for cooling by up to 40% (DeepMind, 2016).

Try this today: check how clean your cloud region's electricity is before you deploy.

Model Reuse and Transfer Learning

Instead of training models from scratch, reuse pre-trained models and fine-tune them. This saves most of the compute, and the emissions that come with it.

Try this today: search a model hub for a pre-trained model before starting a new training run.

Geographical Optimization

Run energy-hungry training where and when the grid is cleanest, or in cooler climates that need less cooling. The same job emits different amounts on different grids.

Try this today: schedule a long job for a time of day when your grid is cleaner.

Efficient Hardware

Use chips with better performance per watt, and keep devices in use for longer: much of a device's footprint comes from making it, and most e-waste is never formally recycled.

Try this today: repair, resell or properly recycle one old device.

Better Training Techniques

Mixed-precision training, early stopping, pruning and quantisation all cut the compute a model needs, often with little loss in accuracy.

Try this today: turn on early stopping so training ends when the model stops improving.

Carbon Tracking & Accountability

You cannot cut what you do not count. Open-source tools such as CodeCarbon estimate your code's emissions as it runs, so teams can report and reduce them.

Try this today: wrap one training script with an emissions tracker.

Environmental Impact Audits

Life-cycle assessments count manufacturing, use and disposal, not just electricity. Counting the hardware roughly doubled the footprint of the BLOOM language model.

Try this today: read how the BLOOM team measured it.

Sustainable AI Policy & Research

Support research on greener deep learning and rules that make AI's energy and emissions transparent, so buyers and governments can make better choices.

Try this today: read one research paper from our library.
