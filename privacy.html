Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

In short: we collect only what you type into our Join form, we never sell it, and we use no tracking cookies.

What we collect and why

When you fill in the Join form, we store:

  • your name and email address, so we can contact you;
  • your role, country, interest, volunteer role and message, so we can send you something relevant;
  • that you agreed to this policy, and the date you joined.

We use these details only to email you about the Green Code Movement, at most once a month, and to reply to your message.

Where it is stored

Form entries are stored in a database run by Supabase on our behalf. Only the LeafByte team can read them. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

Your game progress

Climate Quest saves your garden, seeds and settings in your own browser (local storage). It is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time from the game's Settings tab, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies and tracking

LeafByte does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and loads no third-party tracking scripts. Our fonts are hosted on our own site. If we add visitor statistics, we will use a cookie-free, privacy-friendly tool and update this page first.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, or you can contact us and we will act within 30 days.

Links to other sites

Our lessons link to sources on other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
