Meet Our Heroes

The world has been shaped by courageous voices who have spoken up for the planet. These heroes have inspired change, action, and hope for generations to come.

1. Greta Thunberg

Greta Thunberg, a Swedish climate activist, rose to global prominence at 15 by striking outside Parliament for climate action. She founded the global Fridays for Future movement and has addressed the UN, challenging inaction. Greta's activism reframed the climate conversation, inspiring youth-led advocacy and pushing climate urgency into the political mainstream.

2. Wangari Maathai

Wangari Maathai, Kenyan environmentalist and Nobel laureate, founded the Green Belt Movement in 1977. It planted over 51 million trees, restored degraded land, and empowered women. Maathai bridged environmentalism with human rights and democracy, showing how local action could combat global issues like deforestation and oppression.

3. Rachel Carson

Rachel Carson, author of Silent Spring, exposed the environmental dangers of pesticides. Despite corporate pushback, her scientific rigor led to the U.S. banning DDT and creating the EPA. Carson’s work catalyzed the modern environmental movement, emphasizing interconnected ecosystems and environmental responsibility.

4. David Attenborough

Sir David Attenborough, renowned broadcaster, has brought the wonders and fragility of nature to millions through documentaries like Planet Earth. His narration combines science and storytelling to influence policy and ignite public passion for biodiversity and climate action.

5. Vandana Shiva

Dr. Vandana Shiva, Indian eco-feminist and scientist, advocates for biodiversity and farmers’ rights. She founded Navdanya to preserve indigenous seeds and promote organic farming. Opposing GMOs and industrial agriculture, Shiva’s activism protects both ecosystems and marginalized communities.

6. Al Gore

Al Gore, former U.S. Vice President, brought climate science into the mainstream with his film An Inconvenient Truth. He later founded the Climate Reality Project, educating millions on climate solutions. Gore’s advocacy has shaped global policy conversations and inspired widespread climate action.
