Robustness of AI-based weather forecasts in a changing climate

Various Authors
This recent arXiv study assesses how well state-of-the-art ML weather models perform under future climate scenarios (pre‑industrial to +2.9 K), examining their generalization and limitations.

Climate-Invariant Machine Learning

Beucler et al., 2021
Proposes a novel "climate-invariant" ML framework that embeds physical climate knowledge to improve model reliability across diverse climates. A key step toward robust AI-driven process modeling.
