        How to play

        1. Choose a level. Seedling is the gentlest; Old Growth adds a two-minute timer.
        2. Select a problem, then select the solution that fixes it. You can also start with a solution.
        3. Each wrong pair costs one unit of energy. If your energy runs out, the round ends, so think before you match.
        4. Each correct match earns 2 seeds and shows one real fact with its source.
        5. Stuck? A hint highlights one correct pair. Hints cost no energy.
        6. Spend your seeds in the Hope Garden. Your garden is saved in this browser, so it is still there when you come back.
        7. Finish a round with every pair matched to meet a new climate hero.

        Works with a mouse, touch or keyboard: use Tab to move between cards and Enter or Space to select.
