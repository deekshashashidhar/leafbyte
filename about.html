Our mission

LeafByte helps students and early-career developers understand how AI and digital technology affect the climate, so that they make greener choices in what they build and use.

Technology can speed up climate action, but it also uses energy, water and materials. We think the people who will build tomorrow's software should understand both sides. So we make learning about it short, playful and honest: every number links to a source, and every success story names its limits.

LeafByte is built for students, by students. The site itself tries to practise what it teaches: it loads no tracking scripts, uses compressed images and system fonts, and stores your game progress only in your own browser.
